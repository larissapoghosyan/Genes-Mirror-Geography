Scientific artwork

The Europe → Budapest → park → seated anatomy → heart → cells → DNA opening uses the Europe-to-DNA SVG package. Its original SVG and source notes are preserved. The atlas adapts its camera to page scrolling, scopes SVG references and uses the light Daylight background. The opening geography is an illustration and is not input to PCA; the person, anatomical route and magnifications are schematic.

The external cell and nucleus illustrations are by Servier Medical Art, distributed through Bioicons under CC BY 3.0.

  • Complete cell — original SVG
  • Nucleus close-up — original SVG

The artwork is scaled, faded, and cropped during the explanatory zoom; its internal paths are not redrawn. The original Canvas schematic remains available if an external illustration cannot load. These are illustrative biological diagrams, not measurements from participants.

Animation code

GSAP 3.14.2 supplies the short scroll-following tween when available. It loads from a version-pinned CDN and is governed by the GSAP Standard License. Native scrolling is retained.

The sinusoidal easing functions from d3-ease are included locally, with a browser-global wrapper. Copyright Mike Bostock and Robert Penner; BSD 3-Clause license. These functions smooth the actual scene interpolation, including when the CDN is unavailable.

Project visuals

The helix uses a schematic offset ladder with unequal backbone spacing. See RCSB PDB-101 on DNA and its wide/narrow grooves. Its angular spacing is illustrative, not an atomistic reconstruction.

The opening Europe overview is an illustration, separate from the research data. The homolog ladders, allele examples, numerical figures, and PCA renderer are project code. The genetic scatterplots come from the research data. The small Europe inset uses the geographic dataset and follows the comparative layout of Novembre et al. (2008).
